Our Story & Legacy

Inspired by Love, Guided by Tradition

Established on 26/04/2021 in loving memory of our grandmother, Late Mandakini Nilkanth Kulkarni, our brand is a tribute to her legacy of purity and care. While our official branding is recent, our dairy journey began in 1992.

At Mandakini Dairy Farm, we use modern milking equipment to ensure untouched-by-hand, hygienic milk, preserving natural freshness from our stress-free environment to your doorstep.
